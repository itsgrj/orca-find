# Code of conduct

Orca Find follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Don't share anyone's private information without their permission.

Issues, comments or pull requests that break these rules may be edited, closed or removed, and repeat offenders blocked.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/itsgrj).
