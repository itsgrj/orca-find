Support

Questions

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Sentence understanding uses Apple Intelligence where your Mac supports it; everything else works without it.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. Orca builds its index, reads text in images and understands your sentences on your Mac.

How is this different from Spotlight?

Spotlight matches names and metadata. Orca understands sentences like “the PDF with the blue cover”, reads text inside screenshots, knows which site a download came from, and adds captures, duplicates and clean-up in the same window.

Where are captures kept?

In a folder on your Mac, with the image, your voice note and the text Orca read from it. Settings › Captures shows the folder.

Can Claude see my files?

Only if you connect it in Settings › Captures, and only your captures. Claude reads them when a chat asks for them; your other files are never shared.

Can I search by voice?

Yes. Press ⌥⌘F or click the mic in the search bar, then say what you are looking for.
